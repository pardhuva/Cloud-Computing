2. monitor the resources 
    When you host your project on an EC2 instance:

        CPU Utilization:
        Measures how much of your server’s CPU is being used. Running your app, serving requests, or heavy computation increases this.

        Memory Usage:
        Tracks how much RAM your instance consumes. More users or heavier operations = more memory usage.

        Network Traffic:
        Measures inbound/outbound data. More users downloading pages or assets increases traffic.

        Disk I/O:
        If your app reads/writes data frequently (like your users.db), disk activity increases.

        you can watch these in your cloudwatch

6.  CloudWatch Dashboard

  Purpose: Central place to monitor multiple metrics across one or more instances at the same time.
  What you can see: CPU, Network, Disk, Scaling activity, etc.
  Benefit: You can combine all relevant metrics into one view for your demo or monitoring.
  Customizable: You choose which widgets to add, so it’s easier to visualize scaling events and load together.

2️⃣ EC2 Instance Monitoring Tab (in Console)

    Purpose: Quick, instance-specific monitoring.
    What you can see: Default metrics like CPU, network, disk I/O.
    Limitation:
    You can only view one instance at a time (unless you click through multiple).
    Harder to see trends across multiple instances or Auto Scaling Groups in one place.
